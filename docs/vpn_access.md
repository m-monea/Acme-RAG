# Corporate VPN Access

## Purpose

The Acme SecureVPN gives employees protected access to internal tools, staging environments, dashboards, and private documentation.

## Setup steps

1. Install the Acme SecureVPN client from the internal software portal.
2. Open the VPN client.
3. Sign in using your Acme email address and company password.
4. Approve the multi-factor authentication request on your registered device.
5. Choose the closest gateway:
   - EU-West for employees in Europe
   - US-East for employees in North America
   - APAC-South for employees in Asia-Pacific
6. Click Connect.

## When VPN is required

VPN is required when accessing:

- Internal admin dashboards
- Staging environments
- Production monitoring tools
- Private Git repositories
- Finance and HR systems

## Troubleshooting

If the VPN does not connect:

1. Confirm your internet connection is working.
2. Restart the VPN client.
3. Check that multi-factor authentication is enabled.
4. Try a different gateway.
5. Contact IT support at it-support@acme.example.

## Security note

Never share VPN credentials. Never approve MFA requests that you did not initiate.
