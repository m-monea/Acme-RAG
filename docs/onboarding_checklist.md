# New Employee Onboarding Checklist

## Before the first day

HR sends the employment documents, company handbook, and onboarding schedule.

IT prepares the laptop, email account, VPN access, and required software licenses.

## First day

New employees should:

1. Join the welcome meeting with HR.
2. Set up their company laptop.
3. Change their temporary password.
4. Configure multi-factor authentication.
5. Install the Acme SecureVPN client.
6. Join their team introduction meeting.

## First week

New employees should complete:

- Security awareness training
- Data protection training
- Product overview session
- Team-specific onboarding tasks
- Access review with their manager

## First month

The manager schedules a 30-day check-in to review goals, responsibilities, blockers, and training needs.
