# Acme Solutions Employee Handbook

## Company overview

Acme Solutions is a fictional B2B software company that provides workflow automation tools for enterprise customers.

## Working hours

The standard work week is Monday to Friday. Core collaboration hours are from 10:00 to 16:00 Central European Time.

Employees may arrange flexible schedules with their manager, provided that team collaboration and customer responsibilities are not affected.

## Vacation allowance

Full-time employees receive 25 paid vacation days per calendar year.

Vacation requests should be submitted through the HR portal. Managers should approve or reject requests within five business days.

## Sick leave

Employees who are sick should notify their manager as soon as possible. For sick leave longer than three consecutive business days, HR may request medical documentation depending on local regulations.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week unless their role requires on-site presence. Remote work must follow Acme security and data protection policies.
