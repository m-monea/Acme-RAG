# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Eligible expenses

Employees may request reimbursement for approved business expenses, including:

- Business travel
- Customer meetings
- Conference registration
- Required software tools
- Approved home office equipment

## Submission deadline

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the purchase date.

## Required information

Each claim must include:

1. A clear receipt or invoice
2. Purchase date
3. Vendor name
4. Business purpose
5. Project or department code if applicable

## Approval flow

Expense reports are reviewed by the employee manager first. Finance performs the final review and payment processing.

## Processing time

Approved expenses are usually reimbursed in the next payroll cycle.

## Non-reimbursable expenses

Acme does not reimburse personal entertainment, personal subscriptions, fines, traffic tickets, or unapproved upgrades.
