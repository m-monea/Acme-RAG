# Leave Policy

## Vacation requests

Employees should submit vacation requests at least seven calendar days before the planned start date.

Requests must be submitted in the HR portal and approved by the employee direct manager.

## Minimum staffing

Managers may reject or reschedule vacation requests when too many team members are away during the same period.

## Carry-over

Employees may carry over up to five unused vacation days into the next calendar year. Carry-over days must be used by March 31.

## Emergency leave

For urgent personal or family emergencies, employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible. Emergency leave may be approved retroactively.

## Public holidays

Public holidays follow the employee country of employment.
